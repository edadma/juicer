Bob has been on a one-man crusade against "you must use the cloud" for about a decade. This episode is the case for the opposite: a single rack of bare metal in a garage, well-monitored, serving real customers, for years.

We get into:

- What "good enough" reliability actually means
- The economics of bare metal at small scale
- What Bob would change if he started over
