The children's ministry runs alongside the Sunday morning service: nursery childcare for ages 0–4 from 10:00 to 11:45, and Sunday-school classes for ages 5–12 during the 30-minute sermon (kids sit with their families through the music, then are dismissed before the sermon).

Sunday-school classes are taught by a rotation of trained volunteers from the congregation. The curriculum walks through the major narratives of the Bible on a three-year cycle, age-appropriate at each level. Snacks are provided.

## How to get involved

If you'd like to help — teaching, assisting in the nursery, baking snacks, or just being an extra adult in the room — [email Ben](mailto:ben@gracecommunity.example). All volunteers complete a background check and a brief safety training before serving with kids.
