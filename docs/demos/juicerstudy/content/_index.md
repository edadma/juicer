A reader's notebook. The kind of notes you write in pencil in the back of
a paperback — not arguments, not reviews, just *I want to remember this*
or *what does this connect to* or simply *yes*.

It collects two studies, slowly:

- [**On Reading**](/on-reading/) — what reading is for, how it changes when
  you slow down, and why margin notes are an honest place to think
- [**On Time**](/on-time/) — deep time, geological scales, and the strange
  comfort of being small

Nothing here is finished. Everything here is in pencil.
