title: On Time
summary: Deep time, geological scales, and the strange comfort of being small.

---

Two pieces about time — not the day-to-day kind, but the kind you have
to climb a ladder to see. The Grand Canyon kind. The half-life kind.

Geological time is so far outside the human imagination that most ways
of talking about it are wrong. These are attempts to talk about it
anyway.
