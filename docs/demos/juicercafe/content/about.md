Stratus Coffee opened in spring 2022 in a cedar-paneled storefront
that had been a print shop, then a sandwich place, then empty for two
years. Two of us put the menu together over a long winter of
experimenting in the kitchen of a friend's restaurant. The third
joined a month before we opened to wire up the espresso machine and
build the back-room shelving.

We pour Heart Roasters as our house espresso, rotating in beans from
Coava, Sterling, and Push x Pull on the pour-over bar. Pastries come
from Tabor Bread next door — they bake before dawn, we pick them up
at six.

The "designated working hours" thing wasn't in the original plan. It
showed up after a few months of watching the same handful of regulars
spend whole afternoons here trying to focus. We figured: if we're
already a quiet, slightly-out-of-the-way space with reliable wi-fi
and outlets, we should make that *officially* part of what we offer.

We're not trying to be a coworking space, exactly. We're a cafe. But
we're a cafe that knows some of the best customers we have are the
ones who arrive at 1pm with a laptop and stay until close.

If you want the longer story, come in — the people behind the bar are
[on the team page](../team/) and most of us are happy to talk about
it.
