The room was a print shop until 2021. The bay window and the original wood floor are the only things still here from then. Mike and Andre built the bar themselves over a two-month stretch in the spring of 2022.

If you're hunting for an outlet: every two-top has one, and the bay window bench has a strip running its full length. The long table at the back has outlet strips underneath both ends.
