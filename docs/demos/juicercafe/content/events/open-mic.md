A weekly low-key open mic in the back room. Acoustic-only — we don't
have a PA loud enough for full bands and the upstairs neighbours are
gentle souls.

The format:

- Ten-minute slots. Two songs or one long one.
- Sign-up sheet on the bar from 6pm. First-come, first-served.
- A house guitar is available; bring your own if you'd rather.
- Around 8 slots a night; we usually wrap by 9.

Songwriters welcome — we love hearing originals. Covers fine. The
crowd is friendly and quiet during sets.

The back-room "quiet hours" rule is suspended for the duration of
open mic. Front of cafe stays at normal volume.
