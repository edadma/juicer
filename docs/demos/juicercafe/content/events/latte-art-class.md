A 90-minute latte-art workshop, run most Sundays after the morning
rush. Aimed at home-espresso folks who can pull a decent shot but
can't get the milk to do what they want.

What we cover:

- Stretching and texturing milk by feel — what "wet paint" means and
  how to know when you've gotten there.
- The pour: free-pouring a heart, then progressing to a tulip and
  rosetta if time allows.
- Practice on a real bar — about 45 minutes of guided pouring with
  feedback.

Bring your own pitcher if you have one; we have spares. Six-person
max. $25 per person, payable at sign-up. Email
[hello@stratus.example](mailto:hello@stratus.example) to reserve a
spot — we usually fill up by Friday.
