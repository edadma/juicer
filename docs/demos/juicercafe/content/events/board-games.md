A loose, drop-in board-games night. We pull the games shelf out from
behind the bar at 6, push the back-room tables together, and people
arrive when they arrive.

We have:

- Catan, Splendor, Codenames, Dominion, 7 Wonders for the lighter
  end.
- Brass: Birmingham, Wingspan, Ark Nova, Race for the Galaxy for the
  heavier end.
- A shelf of two-player games (Patchwork, Hive, Lost Cities).

Bring your own if there's something you want to teach. Most weeks 12
to 20 people show up; you'll always find someone to play with.

The back-room "quiet hours" rule is suspended on Tuesday evenings —
we keep volume reasonable but conversation is fine.
