Email is the best way to reach the studio.
[**studio@atelierhahn.example**](mailto:studio@atelierhahn.example)

I usually reply within a couple of days. If you don't hear back
within a week, please send a follow-up — it almost always means the
first one is buried, not ignored.

## What's useful in a first email

- **A sketch of the brief.** A paragraph is fine. The shape of the
  thing, what it's for, where it goes, who uses it. Doesn't need to
  be exhaustive.
- **Your timeline.** Even "ideally before next summer" or "no rush"
  helps.
- **Budget range.** Helpful for both of us; saves a long
  conversation that turns out not to fit.
- **Where you are.** Most commissions are based in Portugal or
  shipped from Portugal; international is doable but adds time and
  freight cost.

For press inquiries, please mention the publication and deadline in
the subject line. The studio's high-res image library is available
on request.
