A demo blog rendered with the **juicerblog** theme. Frontmatter-driven, hand-coded CSS, no Tailwind. Tag pills route to per-tag archives at `/tags/<slug>/`, author bylines to per-author archives at `/authors/<id>/`, date-based archives live at `/<year>/` and `/<year>/<month>/`. The series sidebar lights up automatically inside any post that declares one.
