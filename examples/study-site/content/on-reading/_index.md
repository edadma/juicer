Two short pieces about the practice of reading itself — not as a skill,
not as consumption, but as the kind of attention you give to one thing
for an hour.

The thread connecting them: that *marginalia* — actual notes in the
margin of a book — are an honest record of what reading does, and the
closest thing prose has to a laboratory notebook.
