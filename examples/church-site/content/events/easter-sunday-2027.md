Easter Sunday is the high point of the church year — the morning we gather to celebrate the resurrection. The service runs longer than a typical Sunday (about 95 minutes), the choir leads worship, and we celebrate the Lord's Supper.

If you're planning to bring family or friends, a few practical notes:

- The parking lot fills up early; consider arriving by 10:00 to find a spot.
- Childcare is available at the usual capacity for ages 0–4.
- A reception with coffee, tea, and pastries follows the service in the foyer.
- Easter is one of the two Sundays a year (Christmas Eve being the other) when the church is at its fullest. Bring a friend.
