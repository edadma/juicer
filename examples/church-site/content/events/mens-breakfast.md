The men of the church gather on the first Saturday of each month for breakfast. The shape is informal: eggs, sausage, biscuits, coffee — Ben Carter cooks — followed by a short devotional and prayer for the men in attendance and their families.

Wives and friends are welcome. Kids old enough to sit at a table are welcome too. The whole thing wraps up by 9:30 so the day isn't lost.

If you're new to the church and looking for a low-stakes way to meet people, this is a good first stop.
