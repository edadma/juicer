## Espresso

Single-origin from Heart Roasters this month. Doppio · macchiato ·
cortado · cappuccino · latte · flat white. Oat / soy / almond at no
charge.

## Pour-over

Two rotating beans on the V60. Ask the barista what's open. Ten-minute
brew, served in a glass carafe with a small cup.

## Tea

Loose-leaf from Smith Teamaker. Black · green · oolong · herbal. Iced
chai with house-made syrup.

## Pastries

Daily from Tabor Bread. Croissants, almond croissants, morning buns,
two seasonal scones. Gluten-free options on the counter.

## Lunch (11am – 2pm)

Three sandwiches, two salads. Soup of the day. Most things vegetarian
or easily made so.
