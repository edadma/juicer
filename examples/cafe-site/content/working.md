We built Stratus partly because we wanted somewhere to spend a long
afternoon with a laptop. So yes — laptops are welcome, and we mean it.
Here's the setup.

## Wi-fi

Free, no password — but there's a captive portal that asks you to
acknowledge the rules. **SSID:** `Stratus-Guest`.

If your laptop hates the captive portal, ask a barista for the WPA2
network instead.

## Outlets

Every seat in the back room has a wall outlet within reach. Most seats
along the bar have one too. The front bay window is the exception —
it's a great spot for ten quiet minutes, but not for an afternoon of
work.

## Quiet hours

Monday through Wednesday after 5pm, the back room becomes a
**designated quiet zone**. No calls, no Zoom, low volume on
headphones-only audio. The front of the cafe stays open as normal.

Thursday is open mic from 7pm — the back room is *not* quiet then.
We post a sign.

## Calls and Zoom

Voice calls and video calls are fine **anywhere except the back room
during quiet hours**. If you're going to be on for more than 15
minutes, the small phone booth by the restrooms is yours — just close
the door.

## Stay-as-long-as-you-want policy

Buy a drink, stay for the afternoon. We don't time you out. If we get
slammed and seats run short on a weekend morning, we may ask folks
who've been here 3+ hours if they can wrap up — but it's rare and
we'll always be polite about it.

## Larger groups & meetings

The big table in the back room (6–8 people) can be reserved for free
between 9am and 4pm Tuesday through Friday. Email
[hello@stratus.example](mailto:hello@stratus.example) at least a day
in advance.
