The full menu, in order of how it sits on the bar. We rotate beans
every month or two and the pastry case shifts with the season — so
some items here come and go. Anything with a *house-made* tag is from
our kitchen; anything with *Tabor* is from the bakery next door.

Allergy questions? Ask any barista — most things can be made dairy-free
or gluten-free.
