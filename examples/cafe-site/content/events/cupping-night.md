A blind cupping of five coffees from a single roaster, with a
walk-through afterward — what we tasted, what to look for, why some
coffees did what they did. The roaster usually joins us; in May it's
Mike from Sterling.

The shape:

- 6:30 — settle in, pour the first round.
- 6:45 — slurp through five cups, take notes.
- 7:15 — reveal what's in each cup; talk through them with the
  roaster.
- 7:45 — open Q&A; whoever brought beer or wine shares.
- 8:00 — wrap.

Free, but space is limited to 12. Sign up at the bar or email
[hello@stratus.example](mailto:hello@stratus.example).

Bring a notebook if you take notes. Otherwise just come.
