We're at **412 Cedar Lane** in southeast Portland — a five-minute walk
from the Belmont commercial strip, two blocks west of the streetcar.

## Getting here

- **Walking:** Easiest from Belmont. The cafe is on the south side of
  Cedar, between 14th and 15th.
- **Transit:** The streetcar's Belmont stop is two blocks east. The 15
  bus stops one block north.
- **Parking:** Free street parking on Cedar, Pine, and the side
  streets. Small lot behind the building, accessible from Pine Avenue.
- **Cycling:** Bike racks at the front door and a bike corral along
  the curb in front of the building.

## Accessibility

The front entrance is step-free. There's an accessible restroom on the
ground floor and a wheelchair-friendly path through the back room.

## Wi-fi & seating

Power outlets at every seat in the back room and most of the bar.
Wi-fi password is on the menu board behind the counter — just ask a
barista. See [Working from here](../working/) for the full setup.
